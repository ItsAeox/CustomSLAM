How to record a good test sequence
  1. Start and finish at the same spot, phone still for 2 s at both ends (gives a drift measurement).
  2. Slide sideways slowly at the start, as you would to start tracking.
  3. Tap the floor where a tree should go; tap the same spot again later (gives an anchor-drift measurement).
  4. Then the movement you want to test: turning away and back, walking round, fast turns, bright to dim.
  5. Write what you did in the notes box before pressing Stop.
