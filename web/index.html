Markerless SLAM in the browser

ORB-SLAM3 (monocular) compiled to WebAssembly with threads.

  • SLAM viewer — pick a sequence (EuRoC, TUM-VI, TUM-VIE) or the device camera and watch ORB-SLAM3 live: 3D map, current frame, tracking time and live accuracy vs ground truth
  • Dataset player — runs a dataset sequence and uploads trajectories for evaluation
    player.html?seq=<sequence>&settings=<file.yaml>&tag=<run>
  • AR demo (camera, plane, cactus) — coming next
