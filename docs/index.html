Markerless SLAM in the browser

ORB-SLAM3 (monocular) compiled to WebAssembly, with an ORB+KLT hybrid tracker. Everything runs on this device.

  • AR: place a cactus on the floor (rear camera, hybrid ORB+KLT tracker)
  • AR with stock ORB-SLAM3 tracking (ORB on every frame, for comparison)
  • Self-test: threads, SIMD, vocabulary download (33 MB) and SLAM start-up

The first visit installs a small service worker and reloads once (WebAssembly threads need it on static hosting).
