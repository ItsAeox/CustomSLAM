Camera calibration

Measures the camera's focal length, image centre and lens distortion for the AR page, which otherwise assumes a 69° field of view.

You need a chessboard. Open this page on a laptop or monitor and press Show chessboard, or print one. Then film it with the phone from different angles and distances. Cover the whole picture, corners included. Views are captured automatically when the board is held still.
