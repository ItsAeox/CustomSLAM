XFeat in the browser: feasibility test

Times XFeat (Potje et al., CVPR 2024; learned keypoints + 64-D descriptors) on a 640×480 portrait frame with ONNX Runtime Web, on the GPU (WebGPU) and on the CPU (WebAssembly), plus keypoint selection (top-k after NMS) and descriptor sampling in JavaScript. The SLAM budget per frame is about 30 ms in total.
